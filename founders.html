FAQ

Helpful answers to common questions

The things worth knowing before you pick up the phone.

What makes TAG different from other buyers?
We buy, hold, and grow businesses permanently, not for short-term resale. We are funded by Valsoft and do not require external financing to complete our acquisitions.
What is your acquisition model?
We follow a permanent capital approach: we acquire to hold and support, not to sell. We follow a decentralized model; acquired companies retain their brand, team and ability to make decisions.
Can I be involved after the sale?
Yes — many founders remain involved, and leadership continuity is encouraged.
Do you replace management teams?
No — we prefer to back existing leaders and teams, but we can supplement as needed.
What happens to my employees after the sale?
Your employees remain with the business, with continuity in roles, location, compensation and culture.
Will my company keep its identity?
Yes — your company keeps its brand, culture, and way of working.
Do you ever sell the companies you acquire?
No — our model is permanent ownership with sustainable growth.
Can I speak with other founders who have sold to TAG?
Yes — we are happy to connect you with leaders from our portfolio.
